Random transfigure: pick 1 of 3 of your gem's colour

You put in a gem, the Font offers three different transfigured gems of its colour, and you keep the most valuable. Click a colour to see every possible outcome.

Transform into its own transfigured version (when offered)

This option only shows up sometimes and keeps your gem's level and quality. When it does, these are the gems worth having brought. Spread = best transfigured form − the base gem, at the level/quality above.

The Font pool

Assumptions
  • The three gems offered are different, and every eligible gem of the colour is equally likely. GGG publishes no weights, so this is an assumption.
  • The result keeps the level and quality of the gem you put in, so it is priced at the same level/quality.
  • Results under your minimum price or listing count still take up a slot (the Font can still offer them); they just count as worth 0.
  • Prices are poe.ninja's listing prices, not sales. Few listings means the price is a guess.
  • The pool is GGG's own list of transfigured gems, minus Trarthus gems, Vaal variants, supports and Imbued gems.
Prices: poe.ninja. Gem list: GGG's trade data and RePoE. Source: github.com/DeeKahy/poeGem.
